The Grand Egyptian Museum: Treasures of History ​The Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM) is a modern architectural masterpiece and the new home for the greatest treasures of ancient Egypt. Located near the Pyramids of Giza, the GEM is not just a museum; it is the largest museum in the world dedicated to a single civilization, aiming to display its entire collection of artifacts in an innovative and contemporary manner. ​ Key Exhibits and Major Artifacts ​The GEM is the place where the complete Tutankhamun collection will be displayed for the first time since its discovery. It comprises over 5,000 artifacts belonging to the young king, including the famous Golden Mask, sarcophagi, jewelry, and furniture. This collection is being moved from the Egyptian Museum in Tahrir to be showcased together in specially designed halls. ​In addition to the Tutankhamun treasures, the museum will house: ​The colossal statue of Ramses II, which welcomes visitors in the main atrium. ​The Khufu Solar Boat, which was previously displayed next to the Great Pyramid. ​A vast array of artifacts covering periods from Predynastic times through the Graeco-Roman era. ​ The Museum Experience ​The museum will utilize the latest digital and interactive technologies to provide a rich educational and cultural experience. Through this comprehensive and innovative presentation, the Grand Egyptian Museum seeks to become an unmissable global destination, directly connecting visitors with the magnificent history of Egyptian civilization.
